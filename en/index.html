💬 FAQ Common questions, quantum myths — and your own →
Do I need to install anything?
No. Everything runs in your phone's browser — no login, no app, no sign-up. If you like, you can bookmark this page and finish the puzzles at home later.
Do I need any prior knowledge?
No. The puzzles are made for upper secondary school, but each one explains itself. Curiosity is all you need — and we are right there and happy to help.
Is my progress saved?
Yes, but only locally on your phone. We collect no data, there is no tracking and nothing leaves your device.
