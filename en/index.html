Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your phone's browser — no login, no app, no sign-up. If you like, you can bookmark this page and finish the puzzles at home later.

How much prior knowledge do I need?

Not much. Notions like bit and probability are enough; the pages explain everything else. Box 1 is a warm-up, box 2 calculates in bra-ket notation — and we are right there and happy to help.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, but only locally on your phone. We collect no data, there is no tracking and nothing leaves your device.
