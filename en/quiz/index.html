The quiz pool

Just like the four puzzles of the Schrödinger box: every quiz ends with a four-digit code. Only here there is something for every age.

How a quiz works
Six questions, one after another. A wrong guess costs nothing — you see right away why, and you can try again. At the end you get your code.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to get every question right?
No. If you pick a wrong answer, we briefly explain why, and you choose again. You only move on with the right answer — you get the code in any case. The stars at the end just show how much you got right first time.
Which level suits me?
Children needs no prior knowledge and no calculating. School students assumes school physics, but calculates in your head at most. University students requires real calculations with numerical values and units — bra-ket included. Just try it: the levels are independent of each other.
Is my progress saved?
Yes — locally on this device, without login and without a server. Solved quizzes get a tick and keep showing their code. Everything can be reset on the start page.
Can I repeat a quiz?
Any time. At the end of every quiz there is a Once more button. The answer options are shuffled again each time.
