Questions we like to hear

“Can I ever understand this?”

Yes. Not completely today, but the core is more accessible than all the talk about it suggests.

“What is it good for?”

A completely fair question. Honest answer: for some things we know, for some we hope, for some we don't know yet.

“Do you really believe this?”

We don't believe — we measure. And the measurements agree with the theory to many decimal places.

And if we don't know?

Then we say so. In quantum physics there are questions experts have been arguing about for a hundred years — “that's open” is a legitimate and pretty exciting answer.
