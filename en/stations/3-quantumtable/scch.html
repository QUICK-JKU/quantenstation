The partner

Who is
the SCCH?

The QuantumTable isn't from us — it comes from the Software Competence Center Hagenberg.

Research that arrives

The SCCH is a research centre in Hagenberg in Upper Austria. It works at the interface between university research and what companies can actually use — from software quality and data analysis to quantum computing.

That's also where the QuantumTable comes from: a device that makes an unwieldy topic tangible without distorting it.

Why quantum computing?

Because right now it is being decided what these machines are good for. Candidates include:

Chemistry Simulating molecules — computing quantum systems with quantum systems.
Optimisation Routes, inventories, schedules with huge search spaces.
Security Developing methods that still hold once quantum computers exist.

And to be honest: for some of these points it is still open whether the quantum advantage will really materialise. That's part of research.

Thank you
We owe it to the SCCH that the QuantumTable is here today — and to Alex, who looks after it and will answer any question you have about it.

Can you study or work there?

The SCCH is a research centre, not a university — but it works closely with JKU and the Hagenberg campus. There are regular internships, theses and entry-level positions. Just ask us on site and we'll tell you where to look.
