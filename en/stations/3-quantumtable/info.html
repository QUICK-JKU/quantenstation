Notation

Reading
circuits

A quantum circuit is read like sheet music: from left to right, one line per qubit.

1. The “wires”

Each horizontal line is a qubit. It is not a cable — it is a time axis. On the left is the initial state, usually |0⟩, on the right the end.

2. The gates

A box on a line is a gate — a rotation of the state. You can see this on the Bloch sphere on site: gates are rotations.

3. The dot and the cross

A filled dot marks the control qubit, the ⊕ cross the target. Together they make a CNOT gate: “Flip the target, but only if the control is 1.”

4. The measurement

The symbol with the pointer sits on the far right: the measurement. It ends the superposition, and out come perfectly ordinary bits.

And because the result is random, you run the same circuit many thousands of times. What counts is the distribution, not a single result.
