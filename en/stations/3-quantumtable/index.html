Why a table?

Quantum circuits are very abstract and often hard to picture. On paper we draw lines, boxes and symbols. In reality there are complicated physical operations behind them. At the QuantumTable they turn into things you can touch and rearrange — and the result appears instantly.

That sounds like playing around, but it's exactly how real research works: try, observe, discard, rebuild.

Is this a real quantum computer?
No — it's a simulation, and it computes exactly. A normal laptop can work through quantum circuits of up to about 30 qubits, the largest supercomputers up to just under 50. Beyond that it becomes practically impossible for classical computers — and that is exactly where things get interesting.
