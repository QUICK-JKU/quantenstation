Johannes Kofler

Teaching quantum – and teaching machines how to measure it

Senior Lecturer at the Institute for Integrated Circuits and Quantum Computing; my research puts machine learning to work in quantum sensing.

Field Quantum Information and Machine Learning

What I do

I am a Senior Lecturer at the Institute for Integrated Circuits and Quantum Computing at Johannes Kepler University Linz. I teach basic courses such as “Calculus for Computer Science” and “Quantum Information” as well as advanced topics such as “Quantum Machine Learning” and „Quantum Metrology“.

I am also deeply committed to communicating quantum science and technology to the general public, be it with our project “The Sound of Entanglement” or with popular science talks, in particular for pupils.

My research focuses on using machine learning techniques for quantum sensing and condensed-matter experiments.

Why it matters

At JKU, we teach and educate the next generation of quantum computer scientists. We view our students as the promising future generation of scientists and engineers who will have an impact not only on the economy but on society in general. Thus, we are deeply committed to teaching to the highest academic standard, without discrimination, and with a balance of foundational concepts and technical details. We strive to create a student-centered learning environment that encourages curiosity, critical thinking, and active engagement.

Regarding our dedication for science communication: Education is an absolutely crucial component for an enlightened and free society. "Those who know nothing must believe everything" is a very fitting and well-known saying in this regard. I therefore wish that science, and above all the principles underlying science—such as honest discourse where facts, not authorities, are decisive—gain even more importance in our society.

My research tries to advance the design and construction of quantum-enhanced measurement devices that may – one day – be of use in practical applications such as microscopy.

Fun fact
As a side hustle, I am co-host of the German podcast “Quanten in der Industrie”.
