What's behind it?
To be honest: this puzzle is not a quantum effect, but pure logic — ruling things out until only one possibility is left. That is exactly the thinking that comes before every algorithm. The quantum world is even stricter: some pairs of quantities, such as position and momentum, cannot be pinned down sharply at the same time at all. That is the uncertainty principle.
