What's behind it?

The slider sits between blue and red, and in the middle you can read nothing. Only the two poles can be measured. In a computer this means that a qubit carries both answers at once, and n qubits carry all 2n combinations at once.

That is the core of a measurement: you can choose one direction to measure, but you can't see both sharply at the same time. In between lies the superposition — and it carries no readable answer of its own. More on this in the background text.
